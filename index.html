04 — Craft & detail

READ THE SEAMS

Two reference plates anchor the study: the black-and-white low-top that set the flat, low slung proportion, and the all-white high-top that set the strap and the stitched midsole.

Panels

Six cut pieces on the low-top, nine on the high. More panels means more shadow lines under a hard key light.

Stitch

Single-needle foxing at 3.2 SPI. The stitch is what stops a white midsole from reading as a single flat shape.

Pigment

Suede absorbs, leather reflects. The same hex behaves like two different materials across the two silhouettes.
